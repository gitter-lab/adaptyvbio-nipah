# Expert review
I (Anthony) was one of the experts who was charged with selecting the final competition designs.
The stated criteria were
> Submissions selected by a panel of experts for their novelty and originality

Before making my selections, I wrote my criteria here as a form of preregistration.

## Selection criteria
My objectives were to select submissions that followed a scientifically sound process (i.e. not a random sequence of amino acids), were creative with respect to the methods that are known (to me) to perform well in protein binder design, and would provide information about what types of approaches may or may not succeed at this task. As additional considerations, I value the diversity of this competition and how it attracts international participants from a variety of experience levels. Paraphrasing Chef Gusteau in the film Ratatouille, "Anyone can protein", and I did not want to overlook newcomers to the field. Finally, I prioritized submissions that had full descriptions in Proteinbase.

I reviewed many submissions that I had saved after seeing them on Twitter, all submissions with at least 5 stars, and 10s of submissions with 0 stars starting from the low end of the Average ipSAE distribution. I confirmed my candidate submissions had not been selected by another expert and were not listed in any major prediction markets.
